How we work

We don't start with an app. We start with the behavior. Four questions come before anything gets built:

  1. What needs to happen?
  2. Why isn't it happening now?
  3. What's the smallest action that changes the situation?
  4. What happens when the person doesn't do it?
Privacy

We collect only what the product needs

Architecture follows the problem. When something can run entirely on the user's device, we keep it there. When shared data or accounts are genuinely required, we design for the minimum.

Pace

Weeks, not quarters

The engine already exists and has been tested in the field. You hold a working version early instead of approving a deck and waiting.
